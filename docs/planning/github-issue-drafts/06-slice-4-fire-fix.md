## What to build

Build Ember's Fire Fix: move the helper, aim a hose, spray water, shrink cartoon fires, refill or assist as needed, and complete without a fail state.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Input Intent covers keyboard, touch, gamepad, and one-handed action path.
- [ ] Player can move, aim, spray, and extinguish fires.
- [ ] Cartoon fires shrink with water collision and never create harsh failure.
- [ ] Helper assistance appears if fire pressure exceeds the child-friendly threshold.
- [ ] Completion awards and persists stars.
- [ ] Hydrant/fire/water FX asset needs are recorded in the asset backlog.

## Blocked by

Slice 1 and hydrant/fire/water placeholders.

## TDD notes

Start with Input Intent mapping, fire health/shrink rules, helper-assist trigger, and star scoring.
