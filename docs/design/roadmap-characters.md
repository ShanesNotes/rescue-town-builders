**One-line personality:** The brave leaf-wing pilot who glides through rings of sky-light to bring lost friends safely down to warm ground.

**Silhouette & palette notes:** Chunky rounded frog body with large head, big friendly eyes, tiny pilot cap and leaf-shaped wing/scarf accents in teal-relief #43A29C, body warm-cream #EBDDDA with cool-shadow #3A4D6B leg and back shading, cap in lantern #F4A24C with candle-gold #FFC857 trim.
