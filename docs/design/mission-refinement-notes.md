# MVP mission refinement notes

Status: living design notes for deepening the first three MVP missions before adding roadmap missions.

## Project-wide rule

Refine the MVP by making each mission's core verb more playful before increasing mission count. Every improvement should keep the existing mission id, local-save reward path, keyboard/touch/gamepad controls, and No-Fail completion contract.

## Rivet — Reuse Workshop

Current direction: rescued scraps become invention pieces instead of municipal sorting answers.

- Wrong picks become silly trim.
- Repeated misses trigger a helper snap-in.
- Future payoff should replace text-only invention tests with small animated contraptions.

## Brick — Neighbor Wish Builder

Current direction: house parts are not just a construction-order quiz; each home belongs to a tiny neighbor with a wish.

- Each blueprint has a resident, wish, and completion line.
- Wrong parts become yard decoration/scaffold rather than a dead answer.
- Repeated misses trigger Brick to snap in the needed part while keeping the child's selected piece as playful decoration.
- Future payoff should make the finished resident briefly appear in the doorway/window.

## Ember — Gentle Fire Fix

Next refinement target: fires should feel like pretend visitors being calmed, not generic targets being erased.

- Give each flame a harmless personality/status line.
- Turn extinguished fires into picnic-safe payoffs: steam heart, warm lantern, toasted marshmallow, watered flower, or firefly puff.
- Keep spray-only completion for very young players.
